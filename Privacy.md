## Privacy Policy

This document describes what CryptCage does with your information.

## Information collection

CryptCage does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off the device. There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries in the app.

## Permissions

CryptCage does not request any permission. Your notes are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. To save a copy outside the app, or to open a database from another location, you choose the file yourself through the system file picker; the app only receives the file you picked.

## Data stored on the device

Your notes are stored in encrypted database files inside the app's private storage. The files are encrypted with the password you choose. The developer has no access to them and has no way to recover them if the password is lost.

The app also stores a small preference for the sort order and the tag drawer, and the text size you chose. These settings contain no note content.

## Security of data

Encryption protects your notes against people and programs that only have the file. It cannot protect notes against malware that runs with full access to your device while the database is open, or against someone who learns the password. Keep the device locked and the password private.

## Children's privacy

CryptCage is a general purpose tool and does not collect information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date of the latest change is recorded in CHANGELOG.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the issue tracker of the repository you obtained CryptCage from.
